Reasoning Up the Instruction Ladder for Controllable Language Models

VerIH trains language models to reason about instruction hierarchy. Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) teaches models to resolve conflicts between system and user instructions, with transfer to jailbreak and prompt-injection evaluations.

04 / Research questions

What does VerIH establish?

Definitions, practical implications, and boundaries of the reported evidence.

What is VerIH?

A 7,192-example training dataset for instruction hierarchy reasoning. It combines aligned and conflicting system–user pairs with programmatically verifiable constraints. §3

How does instruction hierarchy differ from instruction following?

Instruction following concerns satisfying requests. Instruction hierarchy additionally determines which request takes precedence when sources conflict. §2

Does VerIH require safety training data?

This post-training dataset contains no safety-specific examples. That statement does not describe the base models’ earlier training. GuardRules are provided during safety evaluation. §4.3

Does instruction hierarchy training prevent prompt injection?

The paper reports improvements on selected evaluations, not a prevention guarantee. Model differences and refusal trade-offs remain. §5

Can policies change through the system prompt?

The approach evaluates new higher-priority policies at inference time. Its results support policy transfer in the tested settings; arbitrary new policies remain unproven. §6

Where can I reproduce the experiments?
